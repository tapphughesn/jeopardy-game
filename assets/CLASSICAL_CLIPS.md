# Example classical audio clips

These five MP3s are 12-second excerpts prepared for the “Name That Classic”
category in `example-questions.js`. The source recordings were trimmed, loudness
normalized, faded at each end, and converted to MP3 with FFmpeg on October 25,
2026. The excerpts are provided on the same public-domain or CC0 terms as their
sources.

## Sources

- `classical-beethoven-symphony-5.mp3` — Ludwig van Beethoven, *Symphony No. 5
  in C minor*, first movement; Skidmore College Orchestra. The composition and
  recording are marked public domain on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ludwig_van_Beethoven_-_symphony_no._5_in_c_minor,_op._67_-_i._allegro_con_brio.ogg).
- `classical-mozart-eine-kleine-nachtmusik.mp3` — Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart,
  *Eine kleine Nachtmusik*, first movement. The source recording is marked free
  of known copyright restrictions on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mozart_K525_Serenade_in_G_Major_1_-_Allegro.ogg).
- `classical-grieg-mountain-king.mp3` — Edvard Grieg, *In the Hall of the
  Mountain King*; Musopen Symphony Orchestra. Musopen released the recording
  into the public domain, as recorded on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Musopen_-_In_the_Hall_Of_The_Mountain_King.ogg).
- `classical-strauss-blue-danube.mp3` — Johann Strauss II, *The Blue Danube*;
  European Archive. The recording is dedicated to the public domain under CC0
  on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Strauss,_An_der_sch%C3%B6nen_blauen_Donau.ogg).
- `classical-rossini-william-tell.mp3` — Gioachino Rossini, *William Tell
  Overture*; United States Marine Band. The source recording is a U.S. federal
  government work marked free of known copyright restrictions on
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gioachino_Rossini,_William_Tell_Overture_(military_band_version,_2000).ogg).
